Welcome to The Vintage Keepsake — a growing collection celebrating the color, design and craftsmanship of American vintage glass, kitchenware, restaurantware and household treasures.

Explore the collection

From the pantry to the dining room

Each piece has a story. This site will document the collection with photographs, maker information, pattern or model numbers when known, approximate dates and collector notes.

Jadeite

Opaque green glass and vintage kitchen pieces from makers including McKee, Jeannette and Hazel-Atlas.

Delphite & Colored Glass

Delphite blue, Seville and custard glass, Depression-era colors and uranium glass.

Kitchen & Pantry

Canisters, shakers, pitchers, utensils, appliances and everyday objects made beautiful.

Restaurantware

Heavy-duty china from diners, hotels, restaurants and institutions, including marked and patterned pieces.

Bakelite

Colorful handles, flatware, kitchen tools, jewelry and other early plastics with unmistakable character.

Barware & Games

Cocktail pieces, lighters, poker, roulette, dice cages, mahjong and vintage entertaining.

Vintage Holiday

Christmas and seasonal decorations, including charming mid-century mechanical and battery-operated pieces.

Milk Glass & More

Milk glass and other collectible forms, makers and patterns that round out the collection.
